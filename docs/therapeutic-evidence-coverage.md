# Therapeutic evidence coverage

Reviewed 11 October 2026. `frontend/src/data/evidence/curatedEvidence.json` contains **86 assessments and 290 regulatory, trial, registry, experimental and identity sources**. The companion `endpointEvidence.json` adds 74 source records (35 diagnosis references and 39 clinical, experimental or regulatory references), giving **364 source-library records**, 175 result records and 37 structured cellular observations across 21 assessments. There are 68 diagnosis-crosswalk entries. Assessment count is not a product or trial count: one intervention can have regional brands, multiple indications or populations, and several publications from one trial.

The current explicit panel contains 103 genes and 181,802 per-gene ClinVar variation rows; gene-symbol query completeness is not a phenotype-specific pathogenic-allele catalogue. The earlier 92 variant files, 119 trial files and 92 expression/HPO gene arrays are preserved unchanged. Earlier atlas exports retain all 726,881 rows; another 88,098 source rows were imported, including 47 new HPO files. The current 10,042 public snapshot records match their archived bytes; the old release's 9,973-record count is historical. [The validation report](validation-report.md) records the audit and exact preservation boundaries.

## Endpoint and population scope

172 of the 175 results have reviewed endpoint, population, comparator, time, role and analysis scope. Three are source-limited and unknown: the initial MONARCH/ADMIRAL seizure result and two pediatric leniolisib results whose accessible sponsor report does not quantify the endpoint analyses. The earlier review investigated all 57 carried-forward single-HPO records and scoped 56; that historical task is separate from the current result total. MONARCH/ADMIRAL extension observations retain the antecedent high-dose subgroup with unverified denominator/uncertainty explicit. Review uses accessible primary publications, abstracts, regulatory labels/reviews, original conference reports and registry records; it does not assert access to every publisher full text or every outcome/subgroup.

The 68 diagnosis-crosswalk entries identify reviewed treatment indications using exact condition references and regulatory sources. A reference allele used to identify a diagnosis is not evidence that it was enrolled, eligible or responsive. Disease, selected HPO and any atlas-cell-associated HPO must intersect within one result scope. This prevents, for example, type-II SMA from inheriting type-I ENDEAR results or type-III Gaucher disease from inheriting a type-I randomized comparison.

Eight measured outcomes remain inspectable as **unmapped**: no eligible single phenotype HPO is assigned to overall survival, heterogeneous neurological response, overall NPC disability/severity or global clinical/improvement scales. These records cannot qualify phenotype or cell-association filters. NPC mDRS and NPC-CSS totals span several neurological domains and do not establish a favourable or null ataxia-domain effect; SARA/fSARA/mSARA and SCAFI retain their narrower, explicitly qualified mappings. `direct` and `proxy` characterize the measurement-to-phenotype relationship, rather than clinical efficacy, cellular causality or therapeutic suitability.

Treatment cards now retain opposing organ outcomes: eplontersen neuropathy benefit versus neutral cardiomyopathy results, tiratricol peripheral biochemical response versus unestablished developmental benefit, and tafamidis cardiac benefit versus unsuccessful polyneuropathy ITT coprimary comparisons. Anatomical and symptomatic responses can also diverge within one HPO term, as in ASCEND spleen volume and symptom score. ETI pulmonary-function and exacerbation results do not establish structural disappearance of bronchiectasis.

Thirty-seven structured cell observations distinguish regulatory mechanism, human cultures, tissue, blood-cell pharmacodynamics and independent animal/model evidence. Every observation declares its comparator and relationship to outcome cohorts. Earlier B-VEC biopsies distinguish seven immunofluorescence participants from three suitable for anchoring-fibril microscopy, separately from GEM-3. ZEVASKYN biopsies remain separate from VIITAL; golodirsen and casimersen biopsies cannot inherit each other’s intervention-specific result. EMBARK muscle expression is visible alongside its failed randomized NSAA primary. Fabry endothelial GL-3 score zero permits near-normal/trace deposits, rather than literal universal clearance. Whole-treatment arrays remain background; independent mechanism/model context does not inherit the outcome cohort, formulation or assay. None supplies exact atlas identity or demonstrated clinical mediation. Missing structured observations remain unreviewed rather than negative.

## What is complete

All **30 legacy catalog products** resolve to an assessment through `legacyTherapyIds`. Kebilidi/Upstaza and Lenmeldy/Libmeldy are grouped by intervention; disease-specific outcomes and regional indications stay visible. Acquired oncology, recurrent respiratory papillomatosis and MacTel cases retain their distinct biology: payload or antigen genes are not assigned as inherited disease genes.

The bounded current FDA inherited gene-therapy inventory contains **20 products**, all mapped in `coverage.fdaInheritedGeneTherapyProducts`. The inventory was checked against the [FDA approved cellular and gene therapy list](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/cellular-gene-therapy-products/approved-cellular-and-gene-therapy-products). EU comparison uses the [June 2026 CAT authorized-ATMP inventory](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/committee-report/cat-quarterly-highlights-approved-atmps-june-2026_en.pdf), subsequent [Itvisma authorization](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/itvisma), and product-specific records. Strimvelis and historical Glybera extend the EU intervention coverage. A historical authorization list does not establish current commercial availability.

Current safety restrictions, authorization changes and commercial discontinuations are recorded separately. This matters for Elevidys, Skysona, Beqvez, Roctavian, Zynteglo and Glybera: a product license, a regional withdrawal and a sponsor's commercial decision are different facts. Each assessment cites its applicable dated primary record.

## Extended mechanism coverage

RNA cases include SMN2 splicing; TTR suppression with four distinct interventions; SOD1, HTT and GFAP lowering; DMD exon skipping; the variant-specific CEP290 sepofarsen counterexample; the individualized MFSD8 example; SCN1A upregulation; metabolic and coagulation pathway bypass; hereditary angioedema; and APOC3 suppression. Clinical endpoints, indications and populations stay separate even when a mechanism is shared.

Additional comparators include CFTR modulation, ventricular enzyme delivery, an enzyme BBB shuttle, thymic tissue implantation, muscle-directed myostatin inhibition and MCT8-independent thyroid-hormone treatment. These expose producer-versus-affected-cell and pathway-bypass mechanisms that a gene-replacement-only inventory would miss.

Thirteen further assessments cover the requested comparator families: three Pompe replacements; agalsidase alfa, agalsidase beta and migalastat for Fabry disease; imiglucerase and eliglustat for Gaucher disease; olipudase for ASMD; asfotase for hypophosphatasia; emicizumab; tafamidis; and the elexacaftor/tezacaftor/ivacaftor combination. `coverage.reviewedComparatorFamilies` provides the exact assessment IDs. The [Xenpozyme label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=01a910ee-a33e-4be3-ac41-322d64c34311) explicitly predicts no BBB crossing or CNS modulation. An absent CNS response is therefore a delivery/indication boundary, not evidence against neuronal disease involvement.

Exact allele restrictions matter. [Galafold](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=66dbd928-0f1c-48b1-a832-54e4abd9f1db) requires a disease-causing amenable GLA allele and remains an ongoing US accelerated approval; assay responsiveness does not establish pathogenicity. [Trikafta's March 2026 indication](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f354423a-85c2-41c3-a9db-0f3aee135d8d) includes responsive or CFTR-protein-producing variants, but its label states that in-vitro transport-response magnitude is not correlated with clinical-response magnitude. Safety and age/weight/onset restrictions are cited in each assessment; the expansion is not an individual treatment recommendation.

The evidence retains unfavorable or divergent outcomes. Examples include negative primary clinical endpoints despite biomarkers, neutral cardiomyopathy efficacy alongside approved neuropathy efficacy, and biochemical benefit without demonstrated neurodevelopmental benefit. Same-trial subgroup analyses are labeled as such, not counted as replications. Registry completion is never interpreted as treatment efficacy.

The scope review also corrected evidence that would otherwise exaggerate benefit. [Kresladi’s FDA review](https://www.fda.gov/media/191993/download?attachment=) found infection/survival analyses uninterpretable; neutrophil expression remains a surrogate. [Genglycos’s label](https://www.fda.gov/media/194364/download?attachment=) reports increased secondary hypoglycemic exposure alongside reduced primary cornstarch requirement, without isolating intrinsic drug toxicity from nutritional adjustments. MacTel structural preservation remains separate from null BCVA/NEI VFQ-25 findings. Rethymic infections are exploratory comparisons with changing survivor denominators and unresolved label/review subsets. Source-specific PHYOX2 estimates, SAPPHIRE dose/population contrasts, current versus historical fitusiran regimens, and later HGB-212 follow-up remain separate rather than pooled into a stronger claim.

## What is still outside the reviewed endpoint corpus

The corpus is **not comprehensive across every approved inherited-disease medicine**. `coverage.remainingModalityGaps` and `coverage.recentPharmacotherapyUnresolvedClaims` are the explicit backlog. The [FDA ARC selected rare-disease approvals](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/accelerating-rare-disease-cures-arc-program/rare-disease-drug-approvals) supplied the bounded recent-product selection above; ARC is not a full Drugs@FDA inventory. Approval dates come from product-specific records, with announcement dates recorded separately. Further indications, genotypes, disease stages, long-term outcomes and jurisdictions require independent source review.

General cord-blood/transplant products, non-genetically-engineered acquired oncology cellular therapies, and other acquired tissue-repair products are explicitly excluded in the coverage metadata. Their exclusion is a scope decision, not a claim that they lack clinical benefit.

## Interpretation contract

`diseaseGenes` identifies etiology; `therapeuticTargets` identifies the intervention's molecular targets. A receptor used for delivery or an antigen targeted by engineered cells must not become an inherited disease gene. Multi-gene syndrome arrays describe recognized etiologies, not evidence that every genotype was enrolled or responded equally.

`phenotypes` contains curated HPO endpoint mappings. Result-level `direct` or `proxy` describes what that measurement represents, not whether it succeeded or identified a causal cell. A result with no justified eligible single-HPO mapping keeps `hpoId: null` and `mapping: unmapped`, its measured outcome and an explicit rationale. Cell fields distinguish disease relevance, exposure, engagement, rescue and bypass. Empty `rescue` arrays deliberately preserve uncertainty when a clinical trial or biomarker does not establish cell-resolved rescue.

A `benefit` trial result and an `indirect` cell conclusion can coexist. Conclusions describe how much the intervention bears on the predicted cell mechanism; approval and functional improvement alone cannot identify the uniquely sufficient cell type. Accelerated approvals based on surrogates retain the need to verify clinical benefit.

## Neurodevelopmental mechanism shortlist scope

`mechanismContexts.json` contains **27 reviewed contexts across 21 genes**, within the larger current panel. The added neurodevelopmental subset covers SLC6A1, STXBP1, FOXG1, SHANK3, TCF4, CHD2, SCN2A and SCN8A. This is a bounded review, not a completed mechanism curation of every panel gene or every allele. All newly added contexts have empty delivery, safety, dose and rescue gate facts.

Dosage-loss support comes from primary human genetic or functional studies and model experiments, rather than constraint scores. The [SLC6A1 variant uptake study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11179425/) supports a broad haploinsufficiency model while earlier model results motivate allele-level checks. [STXBP1 aggregating missense experiments](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-06507-4) and [TCF4 homologous missense experiments](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4736037/) support alternative model mechanisms for subsets of variants; neither is treated as a mechanism assignment to every patient.

[SCN2A patient-derived neurons](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10883610/) report R1882Q gain-associated and R853Q conventional-loss-associated observations within the same DEE11 disease context. R853Q's mixed experimental findings leave intervention direction unresolved. The [larger functional cohort](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11292900/) also found mixed effects and splice-isoform dependence. A disease-only join therefore remains ambiguous when opposite contexts share an identifier. SCN8A gain-channel DEE and loss-channel cognitive-impairment contexts retain distinct disease identifiers; the [systematic isoform study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11383174/) limits binary conclusions from individual channel measurements.

Preclinical restoration is phenotype- and time-dependent. [Early TCF4 restoration in mice](https://elifesciences.org/articles/72290) supports selected rescue, whereas a [December 2025 juvenile-restoration preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12776099/) reports limited later rescue and is explicitly identified as a preprint. These observations do not populate human delivery or efficacy requirements. SHANK3 dosage-loss evidence is also kept separate from multigene-deletion effects and harm from overexpression.

Unreviewed disease joins remain unknown. Shared generic epilepsy identifiers, corneal TCF4 disease, FOXG1 duplication, SHANK3 schizophrenia susceptibility, SCN2A ataxia and untested missense alleles are not silently assigned the reviewed neurodevelopmental loss mechanism.

## Exact allele observations and intervention facts

`variantFunctionalEvidence.json` contains eight bounded examples: CFTR G551D, the individualized MFSD8 insertion, two SCN2A alleles, a STXBP1 splice allele, the CEP290 deep-intronic allele and two SCN8A alleles. Seven have an exact ClinVar mechanism identity. Five are functional examples without a curated clinical treatment outcome. Human phenotype presence/absence, molecular assays and clinical treatment outcomes are separate fields. The STXBP1 functional review leaves the frozen ClinVar uncertain-significance assertion intact; the reported individual had developmental delay without reported seizures.

SCN2A R1882Q and R853Q resolve different observed channel effects within the same contextual diagnosis. R853Q's conventional loss also coexists with a reported noncanonical gating-pore current and assay-dependent excitability, so intervention direction remains unresolved. SCN8A N1768D does not inherit mouse ASO results from R1872W. Model rescue for CEP290 remains separate from the negative randomized ILLUMINATE primary endpoint.

Seven sourced requirement facts and four maturity records apply only within their exact gene, diagnosis, HPO, allele, modality and declared experimental scope. CFTR facts require the published STRIVE intervention/population; CEP290 splice-defect and rescue facts require the QR-110 model scenario. STXBP1's measured defect supports that defect requirement, while corrective-ASO preclinical maturity remains explicitly unknown. Missing or conflicting judgments cannot be supplied by gene consequence, an unrelated allele, another phenotype or a trial phase. None of these examples satisfies every clinical suitability requirement.

## Reproducing the expanded source panel

Run the frozen-source projection before atlas import; selecting new panel genes does not implicitly select a new HPO release or create allele-level phenotype links:

```sh
python3 scripts/refresh_gene_phenotypes.py
python3 scripts/import_atlas_evidence.py --source-dir /path/to/original/portal/data
python3 scripts/build_cell_index.py
python3 scripts/pack_evidence_snapshots.py
node scripts/publish_evidence_indexes.mjs
python3 scripts/tests/audit_snapshots.py
```

The current audit checks 10,269 JSON files with zero errors and the pre-existing MECP2 alias warning. The release evidence contains 235 passing checks: 195 rerun (154 frontend, 14 bundle and 27 Python), plus 40 unchanged previously passing backend checks. Passing checks establish conservative joins and source preservation; they do not complete upstream causal/statistical reanalysis or a worldwide therapeutic benchmark.
